How to fly

  • A D or drag to turn. W thrusts, S brakes. On a phone, use the stick and the thrust button.
  • X points retrograde, against your path. That burn drops you toward a world. C points prograde. M holds a matching burn.
  • When LAND lights, you are slow enough for entry. Plasma takes the rest.
  • Scan a world from close orbit. Mine, then change its air, warmth, and water. Seeding is a promise you have to keep steady.
  • The Behram Loom weaves a chart other players can visit. Actualizing a world does not turn back.
  • The corner mark takes a rating: Fun, Okay, or Skip. Nothing is required.
